# Code of conduct

Be straightforward and be decent. Those are the whole rules, and here is what they
mean in practice.

Criticise the work, not the person. Say what you actually think about a change,
including that it is wrong, and say it without contempt. Assume the other person
is trying to make the project better, because they usually are.

Do not harass anyone, do not make this space unpleasant on the basis of who
somebody is, and do not post other people's private information.

If someone's conduct is a problem, tell me privately through
[the security advisory form](https://github.com/Waiga/follow-through/security/advisories/new),
which is the private channel this project has. I will look into it and act. I
maintain this project alone, so that decision is mine, and I will explain it.
